# Reference: "Still looking for an editor" (Instagram)

Source: https://www.instagram.com/p/DclkKscsRbs/ → `source.mp4`
11.93 s, 1080×1080 (square), 60 fps, stereo audio. No visible person, no voiceover observed.

## What it is

A 12-second self-promo ad for a motion-design/video-editing service ("Kreatively | Motion Design").
It's pure motion graphics: kinetic typography plus mock app UI, cut fast to sound design.

## The argument

1. **Pain (dark act, 0–4.1 s):** "Still looking for an editor." → the viewer types
   "Where can I get an editor" into an Instagram-style search → the search result *is* the brand
   ("Kreatively | Motion Design"), which bends like rubber and whips out.
2. **Relief (light act, 4.1–8.9 s):** "Look no further" (with tired-face + fire emoji) →
   "Get quality edits" (fanned stack of portfolio screenshots, grabbed by a hand cursor,
   collapsing into an app icon) → "Book a project" → "+" button grows into "+ Apply",
   hand cursor clicks it → "Booked".
3. **Proof & payoff (8.9–11.9 s):** a "Request a Project" app screen (in-progress + completed
   projects) → a "Client Feedback." checklist slides in (Audience Retention, Quick Turnover,
   Attention to detail, Main Points Covered, Creative & Trending Visuals) → "Project Completed.
   Thank you for working with us." with a blue checkmark → fade to dark.

It works because it *acts out* the customer journey in UI the viewer uses every day (Instagram
search, iOS-style buttons, cursor clicks): search → find → see work → book → done. The dark→light
switch at 4.1 s is the emotional turn from problem to solution.

## Recurring systems

- **Kinetic type:** words arrive one at a time with heavy motion blur, slight 3D tilt/skew and
  scale overshoot; a word often swaps in by morphing/blurring the previous one. Font: a geometric
  sans (SF Pro Display–like), white on dark in act 1, near-black on light in acts 2–3.
- **Glitch/blue highlight blocks:** small cyan/blue rectangles flash over words as they change
  (0.75–0.9 s, 4.5–4.9 s) — a "selection highlight" / data glitch accent.
- **Inline icons:** laptop emoji inside the text line ("💻 an editor."), Instagram glyph.
- **Backgrounds:** act 1 near-black with soft vignette glow; Instagram beat adds a red/orange
  bottom glow; act 2–3 pale lavender-grey (#E6E3EA-ish) with a soft sky-blue radial glow from
  the bottom; end card white → fades to grey/black.
- **Hand cursor:** macOS-style grab/pointer hand that flies in with motion blur and "clicks".
- **iOS-style UI:** white rounded cards, system blue (#0A84FF-ish) accents, thin dividers.
- **Camera:** constant fast whip-pans / zoom-throughs with directional motion blur between beats.
